One single sentence pitching an absurd invention for a tiny everyday annoyance, written by a Python 3.13 program (standard library only, no network, deterministic) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The Doorway Rewind is a treadmill doormat that senses the exact second you enter a room and forget why, rolls you three steps back out, and replays your forgotten errand in your own confident voice.

198 characters, one sentence. The annoyance is the doorway blank everyone knows; the fix is silly yet oddly workable: the treadmill mat catches the blank at the threshold, rolls the walker back out, and hands the errand back in the one voice they cannot argue with, their own.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
